# Product Philosophy

AutomateDub exists to make Khmer localization feel authored, not mechanically converted.

## 1. Natural Localization Over Literal Translation

Khmer viewers should experience the localized work as natural media, not as a word-by-word translation. The system should preserve intent, emotion, humor, relationships, and audience fit even when the literal wording changes.

Why this exists: literal translation creates technically correct but emotionally weak output.

## 2. Character Continuity Is Sacred

Every recurring character must keep a stable identity, speaking style, emotional pattern, name treatment, and voice across scenes, episodes, and seasons.

Why this exists: character inconsistency breaks immersion faster than small wording errors.

## 3. Voice Quality Defines The Product

Voice generation is not an output detail. It is one of the primary product surfaces. The platform must treat voice identity, expressiveness, pronunciation, timing, and licensing as first-class concerns.

Why this exists: users judge dubbing by what they hear.

## 4. Provider Independence Is Strategic

The platform must depend on capabilities, not vendors. OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Azure, local models, and future providers should be replaceable.

Why this exists: AI providers change quality, pricing, terms, regions, and APIs quickly.

## 5. Human Quality With Less Human Labor

The goal is not to remove humans prematurely. The goal is to make human review focused, efficient, and increasingly unnecessary where the system has evidence.

Why this exists: bad automation creates more work than it saves.

## 6. Memory Makes The System Compound

Every correction, pronunciation fix, style preference, provider result, and approval should make future work better.

Why this exists: the platform should improve with every localized title.

## 7. Traceability Builds Trust

Every line, voice, render, and quality score must be traceable to source artifacts, provider calls, prompts, configs, and human decisions.

Why this exists: commercial users need accountability, reproducibility, and defensible outputs.

## 8. Cost Awareness Is Product Quality

A result that is good but financially unpredictable is not production-ready. Cost estimation, budget limits, and cache reuse are part of the product.

Why this exists: localization economics determine whether the platform can scale.

## 9. Research And Production Must Stay Separate

The platform should move fast in research without letting unstable experiments corrupt production systems.

Why this exists: AI work requires experimentation, but production requires repeatability.

## 10. Build For A Platform, Deliver In Slices

The architecture should support many media types and SaaS operation, but implementation should ship narrow vertical slices.

Why this exists: long-term ambition must not become short-term paralysis.
